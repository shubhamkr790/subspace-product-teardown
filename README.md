## 🔍 Analysis Structure

5. **Top 5 Highest-Leverage Recommendations** — Each with Observed → Problem → Ship Instead → RICE Score

8. **Founder Disagreements** — 5 contrarian arguments with rebuttals
9. **Risks & Tradeoffs** — Full risk matrix with mitigations
10. **40 Ranked Opportunities** — 10 underserved segments + 10 growth + 10 monetization + 10 retention
11. **Strategic Partnerships** — 5 collaborations with impact assessment
12. **Final Verdict** — Scorecard across 6 dimensions

## 🎯 Key Findings

- **The location modal is actively destroying conversion** — food delivery UX on a subscription platform
- **The wallet model is a 2018 relic** in a 2026 UPI-first India
- **Netflix's account-sharing crackdown creates an 18-month existential clock**
- **CRED Money's Account Aggregator integration** is the real competitive threat, not Splitwise
- **Overall Score: 4.8/10** — Promising foundation, urgent execution gap
